Scrollwork Signet Ring

H03 · S999 fine silver $27.71

Old pattern, worn as an everyday ring.

A solid S999 silver ring chased with traditional scrolls and lined with gold at the edges.

The surface is hand-chased with a continuous scroll pattern borrowed from Chinese craft, then the edges are fire-gilt so the silver warms against the skin. The ring is solid, not plated, so it will outlast a plated fashion piece.

Cut in a slightly wider band, it sits equally well on an index finger or a thumb — a unisex piece that reads as jewellery, not as a costume.
